    Next time a review request lands
    
      
    You start from what the change is for, not from the top of the file list. By the time you read a line, you know why it's there and what else it touches.
    
      
    And when you approve, you can tell your colleague why.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Dedicate your focus to what's important
    
      
    You don't need to read all lines when agents are writing the code.
    
      
    An agent writes more in an hour than you can read in an afternoon, and most of it is wiring. You go straight to the lines that change how things behave.
    
      
    The rest is there when you want it.
